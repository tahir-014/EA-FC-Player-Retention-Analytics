# Product Prioritization Framework

## 1. Objective

The analysis identified multiple potential product opportunities.

The goal of prioritization is to determine which opportunity should receive the strongest product focus based on:

- Potential impact
- Confidence in the evidence
- Implementation effort

The prioritization framework used is:

**Priority Score = Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort**

Scores use a 1–5 scale.

# 4. Analytical Priority vs Strategic Product Priority

The mathematical prioritization score ranks **First-Match Conversion** highest.

However, the broader strategic product focus is:

> **Move Moderate Engagement players toward stronger repeat engagement.**

### Why?

The Moderate segment:

- Represents 45.02% of the player base
- Contains 22,511 players
- Has only 27.16% D7 retention
- Shows substantial differences from High Engagement players in sessions, matches, progression, and rewards

This makes it a particularly attractive population for a broader retention strategy.

The first-match opportunity remains an important supporting opportunity within the new-player journey.

---

# 5. Product Strategy

The opportunities can therefore be connected rather than treated as completely separate initiatives.

### Stage 1 — Get Players Into Meaningful Gameplay

Improve the transition from onboarding to first match.

### Stage 2 — Help Players Continue

Provide clear short-term goals and reasons to return.

### Stage 3 — Encourage Progression

Make progress and rewards visible and meaningful.

### Stage 4 — Build Repeat Behavior

Create a stronger Week 1 gameplay loop.

# 7. Final Prioritization Decision

### Primary strategic opportunity

**Improve repeat engagement among Moderate Engagement new players.**

### Supporting opportunities

1. Improve first-match conversion
2. Improve onboarding completion
3. Strengthen progression and rewards
4. Increase meaningful gameplay
5. Improve social discovery

The final product concept should therefore focus on creating a stronger first-week player journey rather than solving only one isolated conversion point.

---

# 8. PM Principle

> **Prioritization is not simply choosing the opportunity with the highest numerical score.**

A strong product decision considers:

**Impact + Evidence + Effort + Strategic Fit + User Value**

The final decision should connect the strongest evidence to a product problem that can be realistically addressed and experimentally validated.
