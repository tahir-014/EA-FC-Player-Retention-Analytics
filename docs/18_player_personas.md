# Player Personas

## EA FC Player Retention & Onboarding Analytics

> Portfolio concept based on synthetic player data. These personas are analytical hypotheses, not real EA player research.

---

## 1. Why Player Personas?

The analysis identified meaningful differences in early engagement and Day-7 retention.

Rather than treating all new players as one population, this project uses behavioral segments to frame different player needs and product opportunities.

The primary opportunity identified is the Moderate Engagement segment:

- Players: 22,511
- Population share: 45.02%
- Week-1 sessions: 4–10
- D7 retention: 27.16%

The purpose of these personas is to translate quantitative player behavior into product hypotheses that can be validated through real player research.

---

# Persona 1 — The Curious Newcomer

### Behavioral Profile

**Segment:** Low Engagement

**Week-1 sessions:** 0–3

**D7 retention:** 15.75%

### What We Know

These players have very limited early engagement.

Their low session frequency suggests that they may not be developing a repeat-play habit during the first week.

### Potential Needs

- Clear understanding of what to do next
- Low-friction onboarding
- Early success
- Simple gameplay goals
- Immediate feedback
- A compelling reason to return

### Potential Frictions

- Unclear next step
- Difficulty understanding game systems
- Weak early motivation
- Lack of meaningful progress
- Failure to reach a satisfying gameplay loop

### Product Hypothesis

A simple first-week journey with clear objectives, visible progress, and meaningful early rewards could help reduce early disengagement.

### Research Questions

- What prevented the player from returning?
- Did the player understand what to do next?
- Was the first gameplay experience satisfying?
- Did the player understand progression and rewards?
- Was the onboarding experience too complex?

# Persona 2 — The Occasional Player

### Behavioral Profile

**Segment:** Moderate Engagement

**Week-1 sessions:** 4–10

**Players:** 22,511

**Population share:** 45.02%

**D7 retention:** 27.16%

### What We Know

This is the largest behavioral segment identified in the analysis.

Compared with High Engagement players:

| Metric | Moderate | High |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sessions | 7.00 | 15.04 |
| Matches | 7.88 | 17.27 |
| Session Duration | 39.88 min | 39.92 min |
| Progression | 8.37 | 18.12 |
| Rewards | 3.04 | 6.33 |
| Social Adoption | 33.73% | 39.86% |
| D7 Retention | 27.16% | 44.27% |

### Key Insight

Moderate and High Engagement players spend almost the same amount of time per session.

However, High Engagement players:

- Return more frequently
- Play more matches
- Progress further
- Claim more rewards

This suggests that the opportunity may be more about creating meaningful reasons to return than simply increasing session duration.

### Potential Needs

- Clear short-term goals
- Visible progression
- Meaningful rewards
- Variety in activities
- A reason to return tomorrow
- Better discovery of available gameplay

### Potential Frictions

- Players may finish a session without knowing what to do next
- Progression may not feel sufficiently motivating
- Rewards may not create a strong return loop
- Players may not discover enough meaningful activities

### Product Hypothesis

A structured Week 1 Player Journey could help moderately engaged players move from occasional participation toward a stronger repeat-play habit.

### Research Questions

- What determines whether a player returns the next day?
- Which objectives feel meaningful?
- Which rewards motivate continued play?
- Do players understand their progression?
- Why do players stop after a few sessions?
- Which gameplay activities are most likely to bring them back?

# Persona 3 — The Engaged Player

### Behavioral Profile

**Segment:** High Engagement

**Week-1 sessions:** 11–20

**D7 retention:** 44.27%

### What We Know

High Engagement players show substantially stronger early gameplay behavior than Moderate Engagement players.

They:

- Return more frequently
- Play more matches
- Progress further
- Claim more rewards
- Have higher social feature adoption

### Potential Needs

- Deeper progression
- More meaningful challenges
- Variety
- Social opportunities
- Continued mastery

### Potential Product Opportunity

The current project does not identify a major retention problem for this segment.

Instead, the product should avoid disrupting the experience that already works.

Potential future opportunities could focus on:

- Deeper progression
- Advanced challenges
- Social engagement
- Long-term mastery

### Research Questions

- What keeps these players returning?
- Which features create the strongest sense of progression?
- What motivates continued mastery?
- Which social experiences are most valuable?

# Persona 4 — The Highly Engaged Player

### Behavioral Profile

**Segment:** Very High Engagement

**Week-1 sessions:** 21–35

**D7 retention:** 67.34%

### What We Know

This segment has the strongest observed D7 retention.

These players already demonstrate strong repeat-play behavior.

### Potential Needs

- Long-term goals
- Competitive depth
- Mastery
- Social competition
- Advanced progression

### Product Consideration

This group should not be the primary target of the proposed Week 1 intervention.

The opportunity is larger among Low and Moderate Engagement players.

The product should also monitor whether new interventions accidentally reduce the experience quality for highly engaged players.

# Primary Product Target

## Occasional Player — Moderate Engagement

The Moderate Engagement segment is the primary product opportunity because it combines:

- Large population size
- Meaningful retention gap
- Existing gameplay participation
- Evidence of progression and reward differences
- Potential for movement toward stronger repeat engagement

### Product Goal

Help moderately engaged players discover meaningful reasons to return during their first week.

### Primary KPI

**Day-7 Retention**

### Supporting Metrics

- Sessions per player
- Matches per player
- Progression
- Rewards claimed
- Second-session conversion
- Social adoption

### Guardrails

- Crash rate
- Session failures
- Uninstall rate
- Negative player feedback
- Reward abuse
- Core gameplay participation

---

# Important Research Limitation

These personas are behavioral hypotheses derived from synthetic quantitative data.

They are not substitutes for:

- Player interviews
- Surveys
- Usability testing
- In-game feedback
- Community research
- Real telemetry

Before production implementation, these hypotheses should be validated through qualitative and quantitative player research.
